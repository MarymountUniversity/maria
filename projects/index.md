## Software & Datasets

**Synthetic Gait Video Dataset**<br>
Synthetic video data for training and evaluating privacy-preserving gait disorder analysis models.
Described in [Giwamogorewa, Ries, and Green (ICDMW 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDMW69685.2025.00010).

**FactRep**<br>
A machine learning resource for identifying and analyzing COVID-19 misinformation across news and social media, developed under the CCI Cyber-Disaster Resilience grant.
Described in Green et al. (SEDSI 2022).
