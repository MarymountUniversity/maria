Applied AI research at Marymount

MARIA brings faculty, doctoral, graduate, and undergraduate researchers together to apply machine learning, natural language processing, and data science to real problems in health care, cybersecurity, education, and beyond, with an emphasis on mentorship, collaboration across schools, and open, reproducible research.

Research areas

Natural Language Processing

Sentiment analysis, topic modeling, and parsing, with a focus on under-resourced languages including Czech, Indonesian, Tamil, Icelandic, and Nepali.

See papers → AI for Cybersecurity

Deepfake detection, identity and access management, open cybersecurity datasets, and synthetic data for doctoral research.

See papers → Health AI

Privacy-preserving gait analysis with synthetic video (NIH AIM-AHEAD) and human-in-the-loop prediction for falls prevention.

See papers → HCI, Robots & XR

Persuasive communication in human–robot interaction, LLM-assisted communication in the metaverse, and VR for teaching.

See papers → Applied Data Science

Machine learning for juvenile justice, misinformation, business strategy, and other problems brought by partners across campus.

See papers → AI in Education

Using NLP to inform curricula, analyze student needs, and map research trends in computing and doctoral programs.
