Dr. Nathan Green has joined the advisory board of the [GO Virginia Region 7 WISE initiative](https://www.gonorthernva.com/post/working-in-synch-with-employers-wise-a-go-virginia-region-7-planning-grant-initiative), a planning grant led by George Washington University. WISE is identifying the AI competencies that Northern Virginia employers need in the life and health sciences workforce.
