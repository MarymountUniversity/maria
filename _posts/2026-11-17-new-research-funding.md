MARIA has new projects underway with NIH and industry support:

- **NIH AIM-AHEAD Hub-Specific Research Grant (2025–2026, $150,000):** *Cognitive Screening Using Automated Speech Analysis: The Effect of Preferred Language in Bilingual Spanish-English Speakers.*
- **Millennium industry research contract (2025–2026):** *Agentic PDF Document Parsing for LLM Ingestion*, combining vision and reasoning models to parse documents for LLM ingestion.
- **Millennium industry research contract (2026–2027):** *Synthetic Data Generation for Tool-Use Pipelines and Model Construction.*
